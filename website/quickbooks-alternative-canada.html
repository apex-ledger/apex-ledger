Thinking of leaving QuickBooks Online?

ApexLedger was built for the firm that keeps books for many clients: one price per person instead of one subscription per company, the accountant's tools in the same file, and every byte in Canada.

What changes for a firm

  • One subscription per person, not per client company. A firm with 40 clients pays for its people, not for 40 files.
  • The accountant's year end lives in the same file as the daily books: GIFI, T2 working papers, workpapers, sign-off.
  • Bank statement PDF import for the clients who still send paper statements.
  • Hosted in Canada with backups kept in Canada; nothing leaves the country.

Moving over

  • Export the Account List and the Journal or General Ledger reports from QuickBooks Online (or the Chart of Accounts and Account Transactions from Xero) as Excel, and import them directly; no conversion first.
  • QuickBooks Desktop IIF files import too.
  • Customers, vendors, opening balances and history arrive with the transactions; reconcile the first bank statement and carry on.

What stays familiar

  • Invoices, bills, Receive Payment, Make Deposit, bank rules, reconciliation, reports with drill-down, Excel and PDF export.
  • A Help & Tutor that walks new staff through every screen on the real page.
