A seat is one person with their own sign-in. Pick the seat that matches what each person does. Mix them freely within a firm.

The four seats

  • Business, $39: a company keeping its own books. Invoices, bills, bank import, HST, inventory, reports.
  • Payroll Unlimited, $45: payroll alone, for any number of employees.

What is never counted
