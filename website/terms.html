The service

ApexLedger provides web-based accounting, payroll and practice software to organisations in Canada. An organisation buys seats; a seat is one named person with their own sign-in. There is no limit on the number of companies an organisation keeps.

Trial and payment

Every edition starts with a free one-month trial. After the trial, seats are billed per person per month in Canadian dollars plus applicable taxes: Business $39, Payroll Unlimited $45 (no limit on employees), Bookkeeper $59, Full accountant $79. Paying yearly is 20% less. Founding firms (the first 20 to sign up before 31 December 2026) pay half price for their first six months. You can add or remove seats at any time; a removed seat stops being billed at the end of its month.
