Questions we are asked

Where is my data stored?

In Canadian data centres, with backups kept in a second Canadian location. Nothing is stored or processed outside Canada.

Is there a limit on the number of companies or employees?

No. Every seat keeps as many client companies as you have, and payroll runs for any number of employees. Only people (seats) are counted.

What does a seat cost?

Per person per month in Canadian dollars plus HST: Business $39, Payroll Unlimited $45, Bookkeeper $59, Full accountant $79. The first month is free, yearly billing is 20% less, and founding firms pay half price for six months.

Can I move from QuickBooks Online or Xero?

Yes. Export the chart of accounts and the transactions as Excel and import them directly; QuickBooks Desktop IIF files import too. Customers, vendors, balances and history come across.

Can it read PDF bank statements?

Yes. Select one statement or a whole year of them; the transactions are read into the review table and the statement's opening and closing balances are checked. OFX, QFX, QBO and CSV downloads work as well.

Does it do Canadian payroll?

Yes, for every province: CPP and CPP2, EI, federal and provincial tax from the CRA tables, WSIB, Ontario EHT, pay stubs, direct deposit, PD7A, ROE, T4, T4A, T5018 and T5.

Can one firm see another firm's clients?

No. Each firm's companies sit in their own folder on the server and every request is resolved from the sign-in, never from anything the browser sends. Before every update goes live, we check that a person signed in to one firm cannot reach any other firm's files.

How safe is my data?

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS only), and the disks holding company data and backups are encrypted at rest with 256-bit AES. Only the web port is open to the internet. Every person has their own sign-in, each seat reaches only its part of the application, and every change is recorded with a name in the Activity Log. The server is monitored for threats and scanned for viruses every night.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. Every company file is backed up every night and kept for 30 days, with a second copy in another Canadian location. You can also download a complete copy of any company file yourself at any time.

Who at ApexLedger can see my books?

Nobody, unless you ask us for help with a specific file. Every such access is logged, and the Subscription Agreement says so in writing.

Do you sell or share my data?

Never. Your books belong to you. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we handle personal information under PIPEDA. When you leave, you download your files and we delete ours.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any computer. Updates arrive on the server and nobody is signed out by them.

How do I cancel?

At the end of any month, from your Administration page or by email. You can download every company file at any time, before or after.
