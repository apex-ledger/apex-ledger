Everything a client file needs

The whole cycle, the way a bookkeeper works it: enter, reconcile, file, report, sign off.

Sales and receivables

Invoices, estimates, sales orders, sales receipts, credit notes, statements, late interest, and Receive Payment to Make Deposit the way the bank sees it.

Purchases and payables

Vendor bills, approvals, part payments, vendor credits, purchase orders with receiving and supplier-invoice matching, recurring bills, mileage claims.

Banking

Import OFX, QFX, QBO and CSV downloads, categorise by rules, match payments, reconcile every bank and card to the cent, with the report your accountant wants.

GST/HST and provincial tax

HST, GST-only, exempt and meals codes on every line; the return built from them; filed periods locked; PST, RST and QST by province; quick method.

Inventory and jobs

Stock at moving-average cost, reorder points, services with prices, projects, tags by department or location, budgets against actuals.

Reports that drill down

Trial balance, P&L on accrual or cash basis, balance sheet, cash flow, ageing, sales by customer, expenses by vendor. Every name on every report opens the record.

Year end and audit

Audit Exceptions, Activity Log, Reclassify, workpapers, CPA review package, continuity schedules, GIFI export and T2 reconciliation, and a Year-End Sign-off with a light on every check.

Help & Tutor

Payroll for every province

Hourly and salaried

Year end

Slips and summaries straight from the posted runs, with a checklist of every deadline.

Your data stays in Canada

A bookkeeping firm holds other people's books. That is a responsibility we designed around.

Canadian regions only

The web service runs in Microsoft Azure Canada Central, with backups in Canada East. No data leaves the country.

One file per company

Each client's books are a separate encrypted file in your organisation's own folder. Another organisation can never open it.

Seats and sign-ins

Each person signs in with their own email and password; every entry and change is stamped with their name in the Activity Log.

Desktop, if you prefer

The Windows app keeps everything on your own computer with daily backups and a second backup folder of your choosing.

No AI in your books unless you ask

The optional AI assistant and the optional voice recognition are off by default. Voice recognition, when on, runs on your own computer.

Built to be audited

Posted entries are voided, never deleted. Filed returns lock their period. Every report traces to the entry behind it.
