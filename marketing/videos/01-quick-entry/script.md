# Video 1: Record an expense in 30 seconds (Quick Entry)

**Length:** about 2 min 30 s · **Who it's for:** small-business owners and bookkeepers · **Record in:** a test company, never a real client's books.

## Before you record (2 minutes)

1. Sign in to online.apexledger.ca and open a **test company** that has a bank account and a Visa card set up.
2. Close other browser tabs and notifications. Set the browser zoom to **100%** and make the window full screen (F11).
3. Have these example receipts ready to type (made-up amounts, fine to show on YouTube):
   - Staples, office supplies, **$56.50** including HST, paid with **Visa**
   - Petro-Canada, fuel, **$89.00** including HST, paid with **Visa**
   - Rogers, phone bill, **$112.43** including HST, paid from **Chequing**
4. Start recording: **Windows + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar). Stop with the same keys. The file lands in **Videos → Captures**.

Read the words at an easy pace. Pause for a second between scenes; the pauses are where cuts go.

---

## Scene 1 — The hook (0:00–0:15)

**Show:** the Dashboard.

**Say:**
> "Keeping your books shouldn't take your evening. In Apex Ledger, a receipt goes from your pocket to your balance sheet in about thirty seconds. Let me show you."

## Scene 2 — Open Quick Entry (0:15–0:30)

**Click:** **Quick Entry** in the left sidebar. Point at the three buttons at the top.

**Say:**
> "Everything starts in Quick Entry. Expense, Sale or Income, or Transfer. Today it's an expense."

**Click:** **Expense**.

## Scene 3 — The first receipt (0:30–1:15)

**Click / type, slowly:**
1. **Date:** leave today, or type the receipt date.
2. **Paid From:** choose **Visa**.
3. In the row: **Category** → start typing `office` → choose **Office Supplies**.
4. **Tax:** **HST 13%** (already selected in Ontario).
5. **Total:** type `56.50` → the base and HST split themselves.
6. **Description:** `Staples – printer paper`.
7. Press **Enter**.

**Say (while doing it):**
> "Pick the card you paid with. Choose the category — start typing and it finds it. The tax is already set for Ontario. Type the total from the receipt, and Apex Ledger splits out the HST for you, so you can claim it back. Add a note, press Enter. Done — it's posted."

## Scene 4 — Two more, fast (1:15–1:45)

**Do:** enter the Petro-Canada fuel ($89.00, Visa, **Motor Vehicle Expenses**) and the Rogers bill ($112.43, **Chequing**, **Telephone**) at normal speed, pressing Enter after each.

**Say:**
> "The next row is ready straight away. Fuel on the Visa. Enter. The phone bill from chequing. Enter. Three receipts, under a minute."

## Scene 5 — See where it went (1:45–2:15)

**Click:** **Reports & Analytics** → **Balance Sheet**, then the **GST/HST** account line (or **Sales Tax (GST/HST)** in the sidebar).

**Say:**
> "Here's why it matters. Your balance sheet is already up to date, and every dollar of HST you paid is waiting on your next GST/HST return. No spreadsheet, no retyping for your accountant."

## Scene 6 — Close (2:15–2:30)

**Show:** the Dashboard again.

**Say:**
> "That's Quick Entry. Try it free for a month at apexledger dot C A. Canadian accounting, with your data kept in Canada."

---

## Tips

- If you make a mistake, just pause, say the sentence again, and keep going. The builder can trim, and YouTube's editor can cut too.
- Keep the mouse still while talking; move it only to click.
- Don't show a real client's name, SIN, bank account number or email anywhere on screen.
