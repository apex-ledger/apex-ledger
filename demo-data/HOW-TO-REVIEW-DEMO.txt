NORTH LEDGER DEMO REVIEW

1. Open a company whose name begins DEMO.
2. Confirm DEMO / TEST appears in the application shell.
3. For retail accounting, open DEMO — Ontario Retail & Inventory.
4. Start in Purchases and follow PO -> Receive Goods -> Supplier Invoice Match -> Bill -> Payment.
5. Open Inventory and inspect quantity, valuation and Inventory/GL reconciliation.
6. Open Sales and follow Invoice -> Customer Receipt -> Deposit.
7. Open Accounting -> General Ledger / Trial Balance.
8. Open Reports -> P&L and Balance Sheet.
9. Open HST Centre and verify collected tax, ITCs and net position.
10. Open Tax & GIFI and compare mapped totals to the financial statements.

All records are fictional test data.
